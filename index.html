About me

I’m a data analyst with a foundation in Mathematics and Statistics (ND and HND) and professional experience in data analytics and business intelligence. I work inside the fintech and banking industry, where accuracy, speed and clear reporting matter.

My day-to-day is extracting and analysing large datasets with SQL, modelling them, and turning the results into dashboards and automated reports that management can use. I translate business requirements into analytical solutions, so stakeholders get answers instead of raw tables.

Before analytics I worked in sales and marketing across two African countries, which is why I start with the business question and the decision it supports. I’m now deepening my skills in data engineering, machine learning and AI-driven analytics, and I’m open to remote roles and freelance work.
